## Boundary Definitions

Board-bound detection means locating the board rectangle in a controlled fixture image. Current measurements only show that the fixture-gated board-bound path can recover the expected full-board bounds for the approved fixtures.

Square mapping means translating image/grid coordinates into chess squares. That is not measured in BLOCK 10.

Piece recognition means identifying piece color and role from image content. That is not measured in BLOCK 10.

Upload/API integration means using detection results in the production upload endpoint or public response contract. That is not implemented or measured in BLOCK 10.

## Known Limitations

- Fixture coverage is tiny: 8 total approved images, split evenly between synthetic and owned/generated real-ish fixtures.
- Real-ish fixtures are still owned/generated and controlled; they are not real uploaded screenshots.
- Real-ish fixtures are full-board, 512 x 512 PNG files with expected bounds of `x=0`, `y=0`, `width=512`, `height=512`.
- Measurements do not cover cropped boards, margins, UI chrome, overlays, arrows, dialogs, camera photos, perspective skew, mobile screenshots, unusual board colors, or varied real site renderings.
- The current confidence value is heuristic metadata, not a calibrated probability.
- No piece recognition measurement exists yet.
- No upload/API integration exists.
- No production-grade or real-world recognition accuracy claim is made.

## Next-Step Decision

BLOCK 10 can move to closeout review after this report.

The next safest technical step after BLOCK 10 is a future piece-recognition measurement/experiment block using approved fixtures only. That work should remain internal/test-only at first and should not change upload/API behavior.

Upload integration should remain deferred until board bounds, square mapping, piece recognition, confidence/failure metadata, and fallback behavior have stronger measured coverage.
