# Detection Real-Ish Fixture Source Approval

This document records BLOCK 10 / Feature 10.1 fixture source approval and candidate selection.

Feature 10.1 is docs/intake planning only. It does not add images, fixture manifest entries, tests, product code, upload integration, public API changes, or recognition accuracy claims.

## Definition Of Real-Ish

Real-ish fixtures are clean 2D digital chessboard images closer to product screenshots than pure synthetic fixtures.

They must be:

- non-user
- small and controlled
- legally safe
- full-board visible
- useful for measurement before upload integration

They may include:

- realistic board margins
- UI-like surrounding chrome
- varied board colors
- varied piece styles
- screenshot-like compression

They must not include:

- private or user content
- raw user uploads
- copied external screenshots unless explicitly approved/licensed
- production accuracy claims

## Approved Source Types

- Owned/hand-created chessboard images.
- Generated screenshots from our own board rendering or owned fixture-rendering page.
- Hand-created approximations of Chess.com-like or Lichess-like boards without copying assets.
- Permissively licensed public fixtures only with a clear recorded license.
- Explicitly approved third-party screenshots only with written approval/licensing.

## Rejected Source Types

- Raw user uploads.
- Copied Chess.com/Lichess screenshots without explicit approval.
- Screenshots from streams, books, courses, private chats, games, or social posts.
- Large datasets, dumps, archives, or unclear-license assets.
- Camera photos, skewed boards, occluded boards, arrows, overlays, dialogs, or mobile screenshots.

## Approval Workflow Before Any Image Is Committed

1. Confirm the candidate is needed for measurement.
2. Confirm the candidate is non-user and not copied from an external site.
3. Record source, style, orientation, and expected FEN.
4. Record ownership/licensing note.
5. Complete privacy review: no personal or sensitive data.
6. Confirm filename follows `source_style_orientation_case.ext`.
7. Add metadata first or alongside the image in a later approved 10.2 step.
8. Run metadata validation before tests depend on the fixture.

## Metadata Requirements

Every future approved real-ish fixture must include:

- `id`
- `filename`
- `kind`
- `source`
- `style`
- `orientation`
- `board_bounds` if known
- `expected_pieces`
- `expected_fen`
- `expected_metrics`
- `expected_failure` when relevant
- `license.status`
- `license.note`
- `notes`
