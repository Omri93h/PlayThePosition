## Limitations

- Fixtures are synthetic/generated and intentionally controlled.
- No real-world recognition accuracy claim is made.
- No upload/API integration exists.
- No piece recognition measurement has been run on approved fixtures yet.
- No production-grade detection claim is made.
- Current measurements do not prove behavior on Chess.com, Lichess, camera photos, skewed boards, overlays, unusual piece sets, or mobile screenshots.

## Blockers

- Real-ish approved non-user fixtures are needed before upload integration is considered.
- Piece recognition needs a measured path later.

- Any future external-style fixture must have clear ownership, licensing, or explicit approval before commit.
